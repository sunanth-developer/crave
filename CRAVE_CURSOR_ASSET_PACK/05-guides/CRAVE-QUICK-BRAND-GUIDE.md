# CRAVE — Quick Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence
CRAVE is a rebellious, adult-oriented confectionery brand built around bold indulgence and expressive visual culture.

## Core messaging
- Brand: CRAVE
- Tagline: INDULGENCE, UNFILTERED.
- Hero: FUCK ORDINARY. FEED YOUR CRAVINGS.
- Supporting: BOLD FLAVOURS. NO RULES. ZERO APOLOGIES.
- Attitude: GOOD TASTE. BAD ATTITUDE.

## Visual identity
- Website: multicolour, energetic, dark editorial base.
- Packaging: individual category colourways, high-contrast black foundations.
- Typography: bold condensed display + clean sans-serif body.
- Graphics: paint splashes, halftone, torn-paper edges, stickers, rough textures.
- Logo: keep proportions consistent; use the supplied SVG as a starter, not final bespoke lettering.

## Product colourways (concepts)
- Lollipops: pink + acid green
- Gummies: purple + cyan
- Chocolates: orange + purple
- Hard candies: cyan + purple

## Voice
Short, confident, cheeky, provocative and playful. Profanity is a campaign accent, not the entire voice. Maintain clean alternatives for paid ads and retail contexts.

## Production caveats
- All packaging SVGs are front-panel design concepts, not print-ready dielines.
- No ingredients, nutrition panels, net weights, barcodes, FSSAI details, claims or legal copy are supplied.
- Do not make health, sugar-free, functional, vegan or other product claims without verified specifications.
- Confirm trademark availability for CRAVE before commercial use.
